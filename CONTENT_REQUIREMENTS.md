# Portfolio Content Requirements

Status: Content-gathering checklist  
Markers: REQUIRED = launch blocker; RECOMMENDED = strong evidence/quality improvement; OPTIONAL = include only when verified and useful.  
Rule: unresolved public content uses [CONTENT NEEDED] in working files and is omitted or honestly reframed before launch.

## Submission standards

### Text

- Provide final or factual draft copy in plain text/Markdown.
- State who approved factual/client claims.
- Distinguish Henry's individual work from team/client contributions.
- Include pronunciation/capitalization rules for product and organization names.

### Metrics

Every metric record must include:

- exact value and unit;
- public display value;
- definition;
- period or as-of date;
- source/system/report;
- conservative rounding rule;
- whether “+” is accurate;
- public-use approval;
- explanatory context.

### Images and screenshots

- Prefer PNG for UI capture masters; use optimized AVIF/WebP derivatives during implementation.
- Capture at native/high-DPI resolution, ideally ≥2× final CSS size.
- Preserve uncropped master plus approved desktop/mobile crops.
- Record product, route/state, date, data type, redactions, permission, and caption.
- Remove personal, medical, educational, credential, billing, and confidential data.
- Never send access credentials, tokens, secrets, or production exports as content.

### Logos and icons

- Prefer SVG with paths/outlines checked at small size.
- Include light/dark variants only if official.
- Record brand-use permission and minimum clear-space requirements.

### Quotes

- Provide exact approved wording, name, role, organization, and publication permission.
- Identify whether editing for length is allowed; preserve meaning.

### URLs

- Provide full HTTPS destination.
- State whether it is public, requires login, or may change.
- Confirm whether opening in a new tab is expected.
