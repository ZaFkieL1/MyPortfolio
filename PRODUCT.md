## Platform

web

## Stack

- Existing scaffold: Next.js 16.3.4 App Router, React 19.2.8, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS 4, pnpm.
- The implementation must consult the versioned Next.js guidance in `node_modules/next/dist/docs/` before application code is written.
- Deployment target is not yet confirmed.

## Users

- Primary: international decision-makers looking for a senior freelance Full-Stack Developer / Product Engineer to build SaaS products, custom web applications, internal tools, or business platforms.
- Secondary: remote employers or collaborators evaluating product judgment, full-stack depth, and production experience.
- Visitors usually arrive with limited time and need to judge credibility, relevance, and contact fit quickly.

## Product Purpose

The portfolio is Henry Gonzalez's commercial and professional web presence. It must help qualified visitors understand that Henry designs and builds complete, production-grade digital products, inspect credible evidence from real work, and start a project conversation.

Success means the site communicates the value proposition within seconds, establishes trust through real products and verifiable proof, demonstrates product and engineering judgment, and creates a clear path to contact.

## Positioning

Henry can take a digital product from requirements and workflow understanding through UX, architecture, frontend, backend, data, deployment, maintenance, and iteration. The site positions this end-to-end product engineering capability through real production systems rather than generic skill claims.

## Operating Context

- The primary evaluation path is: understand the offer, see real-product proof, inspect featured work and results, understand services and process, then contact Henry.
- MediSapience and CEM Nicaragua / CEM Digital are the principal case studies.
- The public experience is English-first for international acquisition.
- Desktop and mobile visitors must receive equally complete, usable narratives.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The portfolio must support a Home route and detailed case studies for MediSapience and CEM Nicaragua / CEM Digital.
- It must provide clear navigation, direct project/contact calls to action, external professional links, responsive behavior, accessible interaction, metadata, and search-engine discovery essentials.
- Services and About are initially part of Home rather than standalone routes.
- A `/work` index is optional after the MVP; case-study routes are required for the MVP.
- All commercial claims, metrics, testimonials, client names, logos, screenshots, URLs, roles, years, and production status details must be verified before publication.
- Missing evidence must be labeled `[CONTENT NEEDED]`; no client, metric, quote, or technical fact may be invented.
- Screenshots must be authentic, privacy-reviewed, and cleared for public use.
- Contact delivery mechanism beyond a direct email link is undecided.
- Deployment, analytics provider, consent requirements, final domain, and monitoring are undecided.

## Brand Commitments

- Public name: Henry Gonzalez.
- Professional title: Full-Stack Developer / Product Engineer.
- Optional compact mark: HG or H.G; the final form is not yet approved.
- Voice: direct, professional, confident, B2B, simple, and human.
- The site must not feel junior, template-driven, hacker-themed, decorative-first, or like a creative-development demo.
- The user-provided reference document is the primary source of truth for the portfolio's visual and conceptual direction.

## Evidence on Hand

- A detailed reference brief defining direction, hierarchy, structure, color, typography, project treatment, motion, responsive behavior, UX principles, and anti-patterns.
- Confirmed real-product names and high-level domains: MediSapience, a medical education SaaS; and CEM Nicaragua / CEM Digital, an educational and administrative platform.
- Existing repository contains only the default Next.js starter implementation; it is not incumbent product or brand authority.
- Final project narratives, exact responsibilities, screenshots, verified metrics, testimonials, client approvals, live URLs, portrait, contact details, and social URLs are not yet present.

## Product Principles

1. Lead with real product evidence, not self-description.
2. Make every section advance commercial understanding or trust.
3. Separate verified facts from claims awaiting evidence.
4. Demonstrate end-to-end product engineering through concrete decisions and outcomes.
5. Preserve clarity, accessibility, and performance across devices.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.2 AA is the minimum target.
- The experience must support keyboard navigation, visible focus, reduced motion, adequate touch targets, meaningful alternatives for imagery, and semantic document structure.
- Essential information and calls to action must never depend on hover, animation, color alone, or custom pointer behavior.
